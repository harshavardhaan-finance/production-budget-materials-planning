# 🏭 Production Budget, Materials Planning & Commodity Hedging Analysis

## Overview

This project demonstrates an integrated budgeting and commodity risk management model for a fictional aluminium foil manufacturing company. Starting from an approved sales budget, the project develops a production budget, material procurement plan, commodity price forecasts, and a hedging strategy to manage aluminium price risk.

The project simulates the responsibilities of an FP&A Analyst working closely with Operations, Procurement, and Treasury teams to ensure efficient production planning while minimizing exposure to commodity price volatility.

> **Note**
> - The business case, company, and scenario were generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI) for educational and portfolio purposes.
> - The budgeting methodology, production planning, material planning, forecasting model, formulas, calculations, commodity price analysis, hedging strategy, and financial analysis were independently developed by me.

---

# Case Study

AluWrap Industries Ltd., a manufacturer of household and commercial aluminium foil products, has finalized its annual sales budget for FY2027.

Based on the approved sales budget, the FP&A, Operations, Procurement, and Treasury teams must determine production requirements, estimate raw material purchases, forecast aluminium prices, and evaluate hedging strategies to protect the business against commodity price fluctuations.

As the FP&A Analyst, the objective was to develop an integrated planning model supporting production, procurement, and commodity risk management.

---

# Project Objectives

- Prepare the annual production budget
- Develop a detailed materials procurement budget
- Forecast aluminium prices using statistical techniques
- Estimate raw material purchase requirements
- Analyse commodity price risk
- Design and evaluate a hedging strategy
- Perform Budget vs Actual analysis
- Measure hedging effectiveness

---

# Methodology

The project was completed using the following approach:

1. Reviewed the approved annual sales budget.
2. Prepared the monthly production budget considering inventory policies.
3. Calculated monthly aluminium requirements.
4. Included normal production losses in material planning.
5. Prepared the annual materials purchase budget.
6. Analysed historical aluminium price movements.
7. Calculated descriptive statistical measures.
8. Forecasted future aluminium prices using:
   - Linear Regression
   - Three-Month Moving Average
   - Six-Month Moving Average
9. Developed an annual commodity purchase budget.
10. Designed a futures hedging strategy based on forecasted material requirements.
11. Compared actual production and material usage with budgeted figures.
12. Evaluated hedge performance using realized commodity prices.
13. Analysed the financial impact of the hedging program.
